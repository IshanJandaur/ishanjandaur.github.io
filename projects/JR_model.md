**Duration:**
March 2023 - Present

**Task/Problem:**  
Develop accurate electrochemical, jellyroll, and cell models to enable the design first Li–S cylindrical cells (18650 and 21700 formats) with proper dimensional, mass, and performance characteristics.

**Approach / Actions:**  
- Used Archimedes spiral geometry to estimated electrode and separator lengths for JR
- Incorporated electrolyte volumes, porosity, and electrode loadings to help optimize number of spirals, electode thickness, and active material distribution
- Conducted tolerance stack analysis on mass and dimensions to understand final energy density spread (Gravimetric & Volumetric)
- Compared against SolidWorks model and data from jellyroll dissections to ensure model accuracy
- Iterated through model to further increase model accuracy

**Struggles:**  
- Creating consistent work instruction/training and place to enter data for SPC analysis
  - Integrated SQL with poke-yoke method for manufacturing
- Solidworks model took long time to update with design iterations
  - Incorporated equations where needed (still requires manual intervention)

**Result:**  
- Modeled cell with estimated +/- 5% Wh/kg spread
- Accelerated iteration by providing accurate model
- Predicted impact of various material innovaations and trade-offs
- Developed waterfall chart to showcase highest impact gains in cell design
- Outlined biggest issues in cell design and addressed next steps

**Skills & Tools Applied:**  
- Geometry
- Tolerance Stack Analysis (Worst-Case & RSS)
- CAD (Solidworks) & Drawings
- Trade-off Analysis
- DFMEA for proposed design changes
